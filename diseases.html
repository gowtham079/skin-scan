GENERAL SKIN HEALTH & PREVENTION

🌞 SUN PROTECTION
  • USE BROAD-SPECTRUM SUNSCREEN (SPF 30+) DAILY
  • REAPPLY EVERY 2 HOURS WHEN OUTDOORS
  • SEEK SHADE DURING PEAK SUN HOURS (10 AM - 4 PM)
  • WEAR PROTECTIVE CLOTHING, HATS, AND SUNGLASSES
🔍 REGULAR SKIN CHECKS
  • PERFORM MONTHLY SELF-EXAMINATIONS
  • USE THE ABCDE RULE FOR MOLE EVALUATION
  • SCHEDULE ANNUAL PROFESSIONAL SKIN EXAMS
  • DOCUMENT ANY CHANGES WITH PHOTOS
💪 HEALTHY LIFESTYLE
  • MAINTAIN A BALANCED DIET RICH IN ANTIOXIDANTS
  • STAY HYDRATED
  • AVOID SMOKING AND EXCESSIVE ALCOHOL
  • MANAGE STRESS LEVELS

ABCDE RULE FOR MELANOMA DETECTION

A

ASYMMETRY

ONE HALF DOESN'T MATCH THE OTHER

B

BORDER

IRREGULAR, SCALLOPED, OR POORLY DEFINED

C

COLOR

VARIED SHADES OF BROWN, BLACK, OR TAN

D

DIAMETER

E

EVOLVING

CHANGING IN SIZE, SHAPE, OR COLOR
